Frequently Asked Questions

What is Model UN?

Model United Nations, also known as Model UN or MUN, is an interactive simulation of the United Nations where students represent countries, organizations, or characters to tackle global issues.

Read our article for a more in-depth explanation.
When does Lake Braddock Model UN meet?

The High School division meets twice a month on Mondays in Room H211, and the Middle School division meets weekly on Wednesdays in Room H212.

Is there a cost associated with joining?

There is no cost for middle school Model UN, however, the high school division has a $15 registration fee and a fee of around $20-40 for each conference attended.

How can I get updates about Model UN?

You can follow us on social media, fill out the interest form to be added to our mailing list, add our calendar to your Google Calendar or check back here for updates!
