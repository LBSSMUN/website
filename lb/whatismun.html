Model United Nations, also known as Model UN or MUN, is an interactive simulation of the United Nations where students represent countries, organizations, or characters to tackle global issues. Delegates engage in research, public speaking, debating, negotiation, diplomacy, and creative writing during committee. Whether you are passionate about international diplomacy, looking to build your leadership skills, or just want to hang out with friends and meet new people, Model UN builds critical thinking, public speaking skills, leadership, and awareness of global issues. 

Delegates go to "Conferences"- weekend events usually hosted by a high school or university. These trips are your opportunity to showcase your Model UN skills in committee with students from other schools and win awards.

There are three types of committees you can sign up to participate in: General Assembly (GA), Specialized Agencies, and Crisis. 

  • In GA, delegates simulate the United Nations General Assembly, where two delegates represent each country. The topic of a GA is typically related to realistic, serious global issues like climate change, education, or international security.
  • In Crisis, delegates are challenged with timed events and crises, testing their problem-solving skills through fast-paced and unpredictable story arcs and shaping the storyline! You might represent a historical figure, a corporate CEO, or even a character from popular media. Test your problem-solving and improv skills as you follow secret directives to navigate the chaos and shape the story in your favor.
  • In Specialized Agencies, a middle ground between GA and Crisis, delegates simulate UN agencies (like the World Health Organization (WHO) or the United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF), NGOs, or historical committees. Specialized Agencies are typically smaller than GA and focus on specific issues, allowing for more creative solutions and problem-solving.

Most importantly, MUN is FUN.
