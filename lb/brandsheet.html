The LBSSMUN Brand Sheet is a comprehensive guide to the visual identity of the organization for officers, members, press, and external partners.

Typography

The official font for LBSSMUN is Oswald. Google Sans Flex is used occasionally for icons around the website. Both are free on Google Fonts. Oswald, Google Sans Flex. Oswald is included in Google Docs. Sometimes, Albert Sans, Times New Roman, Arial, Spectral, Mate, EB Garamond, and Inconsolata are used in place of Oswald in documents. In case Oswald is not available, use any of those listed previously, or the default font.

Color

Style

Lake Braddock Model UN maintains a formal style, and vernaculisms should be avoided. Under no circumstances may offensive or rude language, curse words, or anything inappropriate by FCPS standards be used. All written materials should be respectful to all people, countries, schools, and organizations. The use of proper grammar, punctuation, and spelling is expected in all communications. The club's social media presence may be less formal than other materials, as to be inviting and familiar to students, but must still refrain from inappropriate language.

All communications about LBSSMUN by other parties must remain appropriate and professional. LBSSMUN, its logos, property, intellectual property, or members participating in club activities may not be depicted alongside inappropriate, offensive, or rude content including anything that violates FCPS guidelines, including the SR&R.

Logo Rules

The logo is to be used as directed above, and not modified otherwise. The logo is not to be stretched, cropped, or altered in any way that would change its appearance or meaning. Alternative logos may not be used. Do not place the logo on a background or color such that it is difficult to see. Do not use the logo in a way that could damage the reputation of LBSSMUN. The logo is the property of LBSSMUN and may not be used for commercial purposes without permission. If intending to change opacity, use the unicolor logo, not the "shadow" logo. Do not place alongside another logo to indicate affiliation unless affiliated. The logo should always maintain spacing around it, such that it never appears to be touching something else. Do not use a low resolution version of the logo where it is visible.
